# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or derogatory comments
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The maintainer (currently the sole developer) is responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and may take appropriate action (including temporary or permanent bans) in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies within all project spaces (issues, discussions, pull requests, documentation) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by emailing the maintainer at
[dwb2@andrew.cmu.edu](mailto:dwb2@andrew.cmu.edu).
All reports will be reviewed and handled as promptly as possible.
The maintainer will respect the privacy and security of reporters.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Enforcement actions may include (in increasing order of severity):

1. Correction
2. Warning
3. Temporary ban
4. Permanent ban
